## R1. Deliberate fatal action vs accidental fall

### Problem in v0.1
A confrontation + fall could be explained as Anton losing balance accidentally while Irina merely concealed quality fraud.

### v0.2 evidence design

The helmet camera does not show gore or a face, but it preserves a short physical sequence:

- 01:10:41 — Anton is standing with the inspection recess behind/right of him; fixed scene geometry is visible.
- 01:10:44 — second person’s patched right glove enters frame at Anton’s upper chest/shoulder line.
- 01:10:45 — camera receives a sharp rearward acceleration while Anton’s own hands are away from the guard edge.
- 01:10:46 — impact/cutoff.

Scene reconstruction adds:
- two fresh heel drag/scuff marks begin **in front of** the drop edge and run backward;
- no trip obstacle exists in the corresponding path;
- medical/scene report is consistent with a forced backward fall into the concrete edge/recess.

Required wording at finale:
> The evidence proves that the second person forcefully drove Anton backward and caused the fatal impact. It does not need to prove long-term premeditation.

This removes the unsupported “premeditated murder” claim while keeping a provable homicidal action.

## R2. Conscious C-114 → C-141 substitution vs clerical error

### Problem in v0.1
Mismatched sample numbers alone can be a harmless import/mapping mistake.

### v0.2 evidence design

Positive chain:

1. Anton’s 00:52 photo shows physical seal `K6-C114 / seal 6F-271` on the core sample from K-6.
2. Original lab result for seal `6F-271` is below the acceptance threshold.
3. Sample C-141 belongs to a different control location and has a different seal.
4. Quality system history shows an automatic warning: `section mismatch — C141 source ≠ K6`.
5. The warning is manually overridden in Irina’s authenticated quality workflow with a typed justification that C-141 is the “repeat control sample for K-6”.
6. No repeat-extraction permit/chain entry exists for C-141 at K-6.

Inference:
> This cannot be an invisible clerical merge: the system surfaced the mismatch and a human consciously overrode it.

Identity caution:
The authenticated workflow establishes deliberate falsification within Irina’s workstream but is still treated as motive/concealment context, not as homicide proof. Murder identification remains on independent visual + acoustic lines.
