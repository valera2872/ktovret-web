## 3. Why the early model is convincing but wrong

Initial player model:
- G-6 is on the current plan as the controlled entrance to K-06.
- raw G-6 log shows Anton in and nobody else before discovery.
- Roman lies and creates the lighting interruption.

Natural first theory:
> Roman entered during/around the blackout, killed Anton and concealed the access event.

The case must let the player formulate this without the UI pushing it as “the suspect”.

What breaks it:
1. helmet camera stops **before** the emergency-lighting event;
2. Roman’s actual violation has its own independent E-4/S-2 evidence and does not require K-06 access;
3. CP-6 proves G-6 was not the only route.

## 4. Locked-route reversal — required evidence chain

No inference may depend only on “there is no installation certificate”. Physical openness needs positive proof.

Evidence chain:

- revision drawing: CP-6 exists as a temporary construction passage;
- delivery record: fire-door assembly was delivered, not necessarily installed;
- install ticket: bracket problem postponed installation;
- **01:02 site photo: CP-6 is directly visible open**;
- current operational plan omits CP-6 because it was prepared for the post-installation state.

Deduction:
> The operational plan describes the intended near-final state, not the actual physical state at 01:11. K-06 had an unlogged physical route through CP-6.

This is stronger than a “missing paperwork” trick and passes the positive-evidence requirement.

## 5. Proof against Irina — two independent lines

### Proof line A — visual identity

Material A1: 00:18 briefing photo, full-group, readable workwear. Irina’s right glove has a rectangular green repair patch. Other gloves do not have this repair.

Material A2: 01:10:44 helmet-cam frame from K-06 shows the second person’s right glove with the same repair geometry while the person is within arm’s reach of Anton.

Fair-play requirements:
- patch must be visible at normal zoom;
- player is not asked to identify a face from blur;
- another staff member may wear the same glove model, but not the same repair patch;
- visual relation should become a deduction only after the player links both materials.

Inference:
> Irina was physically with Anton in K-06 immediately before the fatal impact.

### Proof line B — acoustic location

Material B1: electrical-controller event at 01:12:20 restarts V-6.

Material B2: equipment/location sheet places V-6 at CP-6 and gives the three-tone self-test behavior.

Material B3: recorded project-radio transmission at 01:12:31 contains Irina’s own voice plus the same three-tone restart in the background.

Fair-play requirements:
- no voiceprint magic: the radio channel identifies the speaker through normal call sign/name and the content is her recorded speech;
- three tones must be clear and comparable;
- another V-series unit must not make an indistinguishable signal within audible range, unless its event history rules it out.

Inference:
> Irina is still at/next to CP-6 immediately after the fatal window, contradicting her claim that she remained in the surface office.

Independence:
- A is visual/person-specific and predates death;
- B is acoustic/spatial and postdates death;
- either can be challenged alone; together they require an implausible double-transfer/frame-up to acquit her.

## 6. Motive / concealment chain — never sufficient alone

Candidate evidence:
- physical C-114 tag/photo;
- original sample ledger C-114;
- published quality packet references C-141 for the K-6 result;
- version history shows the C-141 substitution entered during Irina’s quality workflow;
- Anton’s note says acceptance should pause until the discrepancy is resolved.

Inference:
> Irina has a concrete professional reason to confront Anton and remove/control the original discrepancy before morning acceptance.

Hard rule:
The UI/finale must never treat this chain as proof she killed him. It only explains why the two presence lines matter.

## 7. Roman’s unrelated lie

Roman’s actual secret:
- after lockout he returned to E-4/S-2 without authorization to correct/retrieve equipment and avoid documenting a procedural breach;
- his reset causes the emergency-lighting interval and V-6 restart;
- he later falsely claims he never re-entered the restricted gallery.

Why this is useful design:
- his lie is objectively provable;
- his action causes a dramatic anomaly near the murder;
- the fatal impact precedes that anomaly;
- resolving his lie teaches the player to separate “suspicious” from “causal”.

## 9. G2 adversarial audit v0.1

### Theory: Roman killed Anton during the blackout
FAILS.
- death/camera stop is before blackout;
- Roman’s violation has an independent nearby-but-not-K-06 chain;
- CP-6 makes blackout unnecessary as an access mechanism.

### Theory: Svetlana used CP-6 and Irina is framed
Needs explicit defeat.
- visual line A identifies Irina’s repaired glove immediately before death;
- acoustic line B places Irina at CP-6 immediately after;
- framing requires both glove substitution and Irina’s recorded voice/location to be explained.
- still require a robust Svetlana location record to avoid “they acted together” becoming equally economical.

### Theory: Oleg used CP-6
Needs explicit defeat.
- route knowledge gives opportunity;
- must lock 01:09–01:13 Oleg timeline with two-source evidence before canon freeze.

### Theory: Irina arrived only after Anton died
Mostly fails but requires medical/camera timing precision.
- glove frame is before camera-stopping fatal impact;
- acoustic line is after impact;
- the final motion/audio must clearly establish the gloved person remains engaged with Anton through the fatal sequence, rather than leaving before an accidental fall.

### Theory: Anton died accidentally, and Irina only concealed document fraud
OPEN RISK.
Current model uses a confrontation and fall/impact. If the camera cannot distinguish deliberate shove/assault from an accidental loss of balance, accusation language must be “caused the fatal confrontation and concealed it” or the method evidence must be strengthened. Do not call it premeditated murder without evidence.

### Theory: C-114/C-141 is an innocent clerical error
OPEN RISK.
The quality chain needs positive proof of substitution, not merely mismatched numbers. Must add at least one physical seal/photo continuity fact plus version-history action demonstrating conscious replacement.

### Theory: CP-6 was somehow blocked after the 01:02 photo
OPEN RISK.
Need construction timing: murder is only ~9 minutes later. Add a material showing no installation crew/equipment entered S-2 between 01:02 and 01:11, or use a second later image/state signal. Prefer positive direct evidence over absence where possible.

## 10. G2 result

**NOT YET PASS.** Three design risks must be closed before player-facing writing:

1. lock deliberate-vs-accidental cause of death;
2. prove C-114 → C-141 substitution was conscious, not clerical;
3. make CP-6 openness at the fatal minute unambiguous, not just likely from a nine-minute-old photo.

This is intentional. Do not compensate with reveal-only facts.
